## Brand & Style

This design system channels an immersive cyber-mystic realm where Edo folklore collides with futuristic festival telemetry. It blends the solemn reverence of ancient Shinto shrines with the electrifying velocity of neo-Tokyo night culture. Crafted for festival attendees, creative technologists, and digital wanderers, the visual atmosphere balances meditative mystery with ritualistic adrenaline.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **High-Contrast Cyber-Brutalism** with **Ethereal Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Nihonga Textures**:
- **Void & Radiance:** Deep abyss canvas surfaces punctured by piercing neon torii emissions and delicate kintsugi repair seams.
- **Architectural Framing:** Structural HUD reticles, celestial quadrants, and razor-sharp metallic borders intersect organic sumi-e ink washes.
- **Duality of Form:** Brutal mechanical precision in layout telemetry anchored by dramatic, sweeping editorial typography and monumental Japanese glyph watermarks.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an uncompromising nocturnal foundation punctuated by ceremonial emissive highlights:

- **Primary (`#FF2E4C` / Torii Crimson):** The kinetic heart of the system. Used for critical interactive triggers, neon boundaries, vital telemetry states, and emissive visual anchors.
- **Secondary (`#F5C26B` / Kintsugi Gold):** The divine accent of craftsmanship and celestial alignment. Applied to badges, premium dividers, star reticle accents, and metallic surface edges.
- **Tertiary (`#2EE6D6` / Astral Cyan):** The cybernetic signal layer. Reserved for secondary system telemetry, radar data, active frequency badges, and celestial tracking metrics.
- **Neutral (`#07080D` / Void Abyss):** An ink-black void ground that absorbs ambient light, allowing foreground glass containers and glowing torii vectors to lift off the screen.

Surfaces scale from the `#07080D` void into `#12141C` and `#1B1E2B` glass containers, ensuring deep ocular contrast without resorting to sterile neutral grays.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional clash between timeless classical drama and high-precision cyber-instrumentation:

- **Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** High-contrast dramatic serif letterforms provide editorial grandiosity. High-impact ligatures and razor-sharp serifs evoke the precision cut of a katana and the imperial scale of festival monoliths.
- **Telemetry & Body (Space Mono):** Functional monospaced typography handles all body prose, metadata, coordinates, and system stats. It reads as direct instrumentation, providing an intentional contrast against Bodoni Moda.
- **Scale Rules:** Display and Headline elements on desktop drop by approximately 35–40% on mobile breakpoints to preserve horizontal line flow while retaining high impact.
- **Typographic Detailing:** Labels and telemetry units must consistently use uppercase rendering with wide tracking (`letter-spacing >= 0.12em`) to reinforce tactical interface readouts. Large semi-transparent Kanji characters serve as ambient background graphic anchors behind lead typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 12-column dynamic grid framed like an astronomical navigation screen:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a 12-column layout with `2.5rem` outer canvas padding and `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet compresses to an 8-column format (`1.5rem` margins), and mobile reduces to a 4-column column system with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Ryōan-ji Negative Space:** Empty space is treated as an active atmospheric layer. Content clusters are isolated through generous vertical padding (`space-xl` and above), allowing backdrop Sumi-e ink flourishes and watermark glyphs to breathe between modules.
- **HUD Reticle Framing:** Layout zones are bound by fine hair-line guides with corner tick marks (`+` and `L` corner reticles) positioned at the outer intersection of margins and gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through atmospheric luminosity and glassmorphism rather than conventional drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Solid nocturnal abyss (`#07080D`), occasionally textured with Sumi-e wash gradients and star field reticle grids.
  - **Panel Layer:** Heavy frosted glass (`rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.72)`) backed by `16px` backdrop blur.
  - **Elevated Overlay Layer:** Deep glass container (`rgba(27, 30, 43, 0.85)`) backed by `24px` backdrop blur and metallic edge lighting.
- **Emissive Neon Bleeds:** Surface edges do not project diffuse black drop shadows. Instead, interactive components cast colored photonic luminescence:
  - *Torii Glow:* `0 0 24px rgba(255, 46, 76, 0.35)` to `0 0 48px rgba(255, 46, 76, 0.18)`
  - *Kintsugi Seam Glow:* `0 0 16px rgba(245, 194, 107, 0.4)`
- **Border Realism:** All panels are outlined with crisp 1px borders using linear gradients fading from solid metallic brass (`#F5C26B`) or neon crimson (`#FF2E4C`) down into transparent void edges (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a razor-sharp, zero-radius geometry (`roundedness: 0`):

- **Katana Edges:** All interactive cards, button containers, badges, and modals have precise 90-degree corners (`0px` border-radius) evoking clean blade cuts and shrine architectural timber joints.
- **Chamfered Cuts:** Advanced containers feature 45-degree angled chamfer cutouts on top-right or diagonal corner pairs (via CSS clip-paths), referencing tactical HUD elements and origami paper folds.
- **Linear Partitions:** Dividers are executed as ultra-thin `1px` lines accented with diamond or crosshair center nodes.

## Components

### Buttons & Actuators
- **Primary Cyber-Torii Button:** Sharp rectangular button with a solid `#FF2E4C` fill, `#07080D` Space Mono bold text, and a constant subtle outer glow. Hover activates an expanded crimson aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 28px rgba(255, 46, 76, 0.6)`) and an internal micro-scanline transition.
- **Kintsugi Ghost Button:** Transparent glass container with a `1px` border of `#F5C26B`. Text is rendered in crisp gold uppercase monospace. Hover causes gold leaf ink wash overlays to flood the background with a 15% opacity tint.

### Cards & Shrines
- Constructed with `rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.72)` background fills and `16px` backdrop filters.
- Enclosed with a `1px` boundary displaying corner HUD ticks (`+` markers in gold).
- Large watermarked Japanese kanji glyphs (e.g., 侍, 祭, 影) sit locked to the background at 4% to 8% opacity.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Monospaced metadata badges encased in fine `1px` borders (`#2EE6D6` or `#F5C26B`).
- Prefixed with small pulsing status dots (`●`) or coordinate symbols (`[SYS // OK]`).
- Strict uppercase, zero border radius, and tight horizontal padding (`0.5rem`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Recessed void inputs (`#0D0E15`) with a single crisp bottom border or full metallic hairline outline.
- Active focus state immediately ignites a bottom Torii Crimson glow accompanied by a monospace field status readout (`[INPUT_LOCKED]`).
- Placeholder text sits in muted slate monospace at 40% opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square, unrounded indicators.
- Unchecked: `1px` outline of `#F5C26B` against the dark glass backing.
- Checked: Inverted solid `#FF2E4C` center core framed by a secondary perimeter reticle ring.

### Bespoke Celestial Festival Components
- **HUD Reticle Targeters:** Circular and crosshair SVG overlays that track cursor positions or frame key festival media.
- **Torii Gateway Portals:** Modular header and hero frames styled after traditional two-pillar shrine architecture, built with glowing neon beams.
- **Sumi-e Dynamic Divider:** Horizontal divider lines that blend mechanical raster dashes into an organic, brush-style ink streak.
